Works with your tools.
On your computer.

Brain’s CLI brings approved meetings, transcripts, and decisions into the tools you use. Any local harness that runs terminal commands can work with Brain.

Or connect an MCP-compatible client directly to its local server.

Connect your tools
Brain CLILocal · scope-bound

Search approved meeting context

brain search "release decision" --scope Personal --json

Connect an MCP client

brain mcp --scope Personal --print-config claude
Real CLI syntax. Replace Personal with your scope.
Find your platform’s launcher

Standard read access includes approved records in the selected scope. Your harness’s model settings determine whether retrieved text leaves your computer.

On your computer.
On your terms.

Your recordings and notes stay in your local account. Speech, summaries, and questions can run locally after the tools are downloaded.

You choose whether to connect an online service. Connected services and agents may receive the text you share with them.
