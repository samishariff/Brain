What’s new in 0.2.75

Stops unnecessary background AI load and adds Use built-in AI only in Settings → AI & Summaries. Brain bounds speaker-identification requests, remembers progress and retry limits, and keeps local speaker clues available while a connection is offline.

Built-in fallback now covers all AI features with compatible request sizes. Turning on built-in-only mode cancels other AI connections and pauses their polling while preserving your saved connection settings. Existing unconfirmed speaker suggestions receive one bounded refresh after upgrading.

What’s new in 0.2.74

Install the latest Brain update from 0.2.72 without being rolled back after the new app opens.

Fixed a compatibility error between the older updater and the new app’s startup confirmation. This update includes all the improvements listed below for 0.2.73, including the Speak resetting fix.

What’s new in 0.2.73

Review actions with their context, keep your place while naming voices, and dictate again without getting stuck at “Speak is resetting.”

What's new

  • Actions have clearer titles and a detail view with the supporting transcript. Group them by due date or meeting, and review items that need context before using them.
  • Browse Completed and Dismissed actions separately in History, restore them, and undo your changes. You can also review older action suggestions through an explicit cleanup step.
  • People keeps its loaded state while you review voices. Sample checks show progress and offer Pause and Resume, with more consistent undo for naming decisions.
  • Brain checks past meetings for known voices when it is not recording or processing, and checks again after learning more voices. Control this in Settings under Speaker names.
  • Turn on Daily update check in Settings to hear when a release is available, read What's new, and choose Remind me tomorrow or Skip this version. Downloads and installation remain your choice.
  • Connected assistants with review access can follow live transcripts, read draft reports, help correct speakers, edit reports and associate calendar context through MCP and the command line. Your reports still wait for approval.
  • Reports have clearer editing and discard controls, and bulk Trash in Today can be undone.

Fixes

  • Speak checks the microphone when you press, fixing stale Bluetooth microphone setup after a call. A stuck cleanup no longer rejects every press with “Speak is resetting. Try again.”
  • Speak accounts for empty-field placeholders in Claude and Slack and gives the editor longer to confirm insertion, reducing false “Check the field” messages when your words have landed.
  • Model downloads verify the expected files and safely handle interrupted downloads. Brain-created local data and backups have tighter file permissions, and routine voice-merge logs omit personal names.

Under the hood

Your library upgrades from schema 42 to 44 on first launch, with a backup before migration. Existing recordings and approved reports are retained.

What Brain includes

  • Automatic recording of Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls after you say yes, and any conversation on request.
  • A live transcript during the call, grouped into readable paragraphs, with your voice and the call audio kept separate.
  • A meeting helper you can ask while the call is still going, or when you come back to a saved meeting.
  • A transcript that shows who said what, and an editable summary after the call, with decisions and next steps linked to what was said.
  • Voice learning, so the people you meet with are named on later transcripts.
  • A searchable meeting library, daily backups of your meeting library, and updates that install themselves.

What’s still being checked

Brain is an early preview for practice and feedback. These are the known rough edges:

  • On rare calls the live transcript shows only your side, or stops partway through. The recording continues and the full transcript is written when the call ends. If it happens, tell Sami which call it was.
  • Long calls on a warm Mac: Brain slows the live transcript down to keep the recording safe. The full transcript after the call is unaffected.
  • Live text works best when the call runs through headphones or a headset. Through speakers, your own voice can be picked up twice.
  • Names on transcripts improve as Brain hears people more often. Early on, expect some unnamed speakers.

If you installed version 0.2.52, download 0.2.53 from the Mac setup page once. From 0.2.53 onward, Brain updates itself.

Before your first meeting

Start with a short practice recording of yourself. Tell everyone before recording a call, and check that you can hear both sides afterward.

The first recording needs an internet connection so Brain can download the speech-recognition files it runs on your Mac. After that, speech recognition and summaries run on your Mac without sending meeting content to an online service, unless you connect one in Settings.

Transcripts, summaries, and helper answers can miss details. Review anything important before relying on it or sharing it, and keep another copy of recordings and notes you want to retain.

Updates

Turn on Daily update check in Settings to hear when a newer version is available. Download and install when you choose. Choose Install and Relaunch when you are not recording; your meetings are kept. If an update ever fails, Brain restores the version you had.

You can always check by hand with Brain › Check for Updates…, or download the latest version from the Mac setup page.
