From your first setup to finding a saved conversation. Here’s where to begin.

Install and set up Brain

Check the Brain home page for download availability. When a download is available, open the setup file and follow the instructions.

Windows may show a security warning because Sami shares Brain with a personal signing certificate. The first installation asks an administrator to trust Sami Shariff’s app certificate. If you’re unsure about a prompt, ask Sami or the person who manages your PC. You do not need to turn off Windows security or enable developer mode.

Open Brain from your Start menu and choose Set up Brain. Brain guides you through downloading the speech and summary tools and checking your microphone. Keep your internet connection available until the downloads finish.

If setup is interrupted, open Brain and try again. Completed downloads are kept.

Start with a short recording

  1. Choose Record and select your microphone.
  2. Speak for a few seconds, then stop and listen back.
  3. For a call, check that the recording includes the computer audio too.

Tell everyone before you record. Use a practice conversation first, and keep another copy of anything important while you try the preview.

After a meeting, review the transcript and summary. Speech recognition can miss names or details, so check the parts you plan to use or share.

Add your calendar

Open Settings, choose Connections, then Your calendars. You can add a calendar link or import a calendar file.

Google and Microsoft calendar sign-in is not enabled in this preview. Keep private calendar links out of screenshots and public messages.

Keep Brain up to date

The website does not offer an update until a release is ready. The installed app’s update choices are still being tested; keep a full backup before trying an update.

Open Settings to check. If an update is available, choose Update and restart when your work is finished. You can also choose automatic installation when you exit after saving.

Closing the window may leave Brain running near the clock. Choose Exit Brain to close it fully. If an update fails, keep your existing app and ask Sami for help before uninstalling anything.

Keep a copy of your work

Daily backups protect your meeting records. To include your recordings, choose Back up everything. Save a full backup to a separate drive if the meetings matter to you.

Backups can contain private conversations. Choose a storage location you trust, and keep the original app folder when moving from an earlier preview until you’ve checked the imported meetings.

If something goes wrong

You can’t hear a recording

Stop recording and check your microphone connection and Windows microphone privacy settings. Make another short recording before continuing.

Brain closed unexpectedly

Reopen Brain and follow any recovery instructions before starting another recording.

You need a hand

Contact Sami through your usual private channel. Explain what you were trying to do and what happened. If Brain opens, go to Help & about and choose Save diagnostic report. Review it before sharing.

Keep recordings, meeting text, passwords, sign-in codes, account details, and private calendar links out of public messages. If you can’t open Brain, tell Sami your Windows version and what happened just before the problem.
