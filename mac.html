Install Brain

  1. Download Brain using the button above and open the disk image.
  2. Drag Brain into your Applications folder, then open it from there.
  3. Brain is signed and notarized with Apple, so macOS opens it without a security warning. If you are asked to confirm, choose Open.

The first time you record, macOS asks for permission to use your microphone and, for calls, to record system audio so Brain can hear the other people on the call. Brain also downloads its on-device speech models the first time you need them; keep your internet connection available until that finishes.

Brain needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. It does not run on Intel Macs or older versions of macOS.

Start with a short recording

  1. Open Brain, choose Record, and make a short practice recording of yourself.
  2. Join a Zoom or Teams call. Brain notices the call and offers to record it — accept, and the live transcript starts in the Record view, with your voice and the call audio kept separate.
  3. When the call ends, Brain writes the full transcript and a summary. Review the decisions and next steps, keep the ones you agree with, and find the meeting again later in your library.

Tell everyone before you record. Speech recognition can miss names and details, so check anything you plan to rely on or share.

What Brain does

  • Records Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls automatically after you say yes, and any conversation on request.
  • Shows a live transcript during the call, grouped into readable paragraphs, with a meeting assistant you can ask while you are still talking.
  • Writes a speaker-attributed transcript and an editable summary after the call, with decisions and next steps linked to what was said.
  • Learns the voices of the people you meet with, so names appear on later transcripts.
  • Keeps a searchable library of your meetings.

Your meetings stay on your Mac

Recordings, transcripts, and notes are stored in your user account on this Mac. Speech recognition and the meeting summary run on your Mac; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to connect an online AI service in Settings. If you do, that service may receive meeting text and your questions.

Updates

Brain updates itself. Choose Brain › Check for Updates… (or the same button in Settings). Brain looks for a newer version, downloads it, checks its signature, and replaces itself in your Applications folder when you choose Install and Relaunch. Your meetings are kept.

Brain won't install an update while a recording is running. If an update ever fails, Brain restores the version you had; you can also download the latest version from this page at any time.

If something goes wrong

Brain did not notice a call

Choose Record and start the recording yourself. Check that macOS has allowed Brain to use the microphone and to record system audio in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The live transcript stopped

The recording keeps going and the full transcript is written when the call ends. Tell Sami which call it was and roughly when the text stopped.

You need a hand

Contact Sami through your usual private channel and say what you were trying to do and what happened. Keep recordings, meeting text, passwords, and account details out of public messages.
